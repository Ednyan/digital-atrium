# Drop and paste

Drag files or links onto the canvas, or paste them, and each one becomes a trace.
While you drag, the canvas says **DROP FILE TO CREATE TRACE** or **DROP LINK TO
CREATE TRACE**.

![A picture dragged from the computer and dropped on the canvas](../images/drop-file.webp)

## What each becomes

| You bring in | It becomes |
|---|---|
| A picture | An image trace |
| A video or a sound file | A video or audio trace that plays in place |
| A PDF or a .pptx slide deck | A document trace, a page at a time, with arrows to turn the pages |
| A spreadsheet | A sheet trace, read in the browser |
| A link to a video, web page or pin | An embed trace |
| Text | A text trace |

## Bring things in

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| A file or a link where you want it | Drag it onto the canvas and let go there |
| What you copied | Press **Ctrl+V** over the canvas |
| A copied picture or link, from the menu | Right-click the canvas > **Paste Image** or **Paste as Embed** |
| A PDF as one picture per page | Drop it, then use **Extract Pages** on the trace |

## Several at once

Drop or paste several files together and they're laid out around where you let
go: in a square, or in a circle.

To choose which, open your preferences and set **Shape for batch placement** to
**square** or **circle**. It doesn't change **Reorganize Selected**.

## Good to know

- A folder can't be dropped: open it and drop the files inside.
- In an online atrium, one file can be 25 MB at most, and all your online atriums share 50 MB. See [Storage and limits](../keeping-and-moving/storage-and-limits.md).
- A link or an embed keeps only its address, so it takes almost no space.
- If nothing usable is in what you dropped, the app says so: "Nothing droppable in that".
- **Ctrl+V** pastes traces you copied in the atrium too, and **Ctrl+Alt+V** pastes a copied style.
