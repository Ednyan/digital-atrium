# The quick bar

The quick bar is the column of tools on the left edge of an atrium. Pick a tool,
then click or drag on the canvas.

## The tools

| Tool | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Select** | **1** | Pick, move and resize traces |
| **Selection** | **M** | Drag an area or a lasso round several traces to select them |
| **Move the view** | **H** | Drag to move around the canvas |
| **Text in a box** | **2** | Place text, then type |
| **Shapes** | **3** | Place a rectangle, circle or other shape |
| **Connect** | **C** | Draw a thread from one trace to another |
| **Path** | **4** | Place a line, then add its points |
| **Draw** | **5** | Draw freehand on the canvas |
| **Image** | **6** | Add a picture from your files |
| **Embed** | **7** | Add a video, web page or other link |
| **Frame** | **8** | Place a titled box that holds traces |
| **Other Traces** | | The trace types not on the bar |
| **Laser pointer** | **K** | Point at things for the people in the room; nothing is placed |
| **Your Pinterest Boards** | | Import pins from your boards |

## Place a trace

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| The usual size | Pick the tool, then click the canvas |
| A size of your own | Pick the tool, then drag on the canvas |
| An even square or circle | Hold **Shift** while you drag |
| Stop placing | Press **Esc**, or **1** for **Select** |

**Shapes**, **Path** and **Frame** stay on after you place one, so you can place
several. The trace you placed opens in the Customization panel.

**Draw**, **Image**, **Embed** and **Your Pinterest Boards** don't wait for a click
on the canvas: they open at once.

## Change the bar

What doesn't fit goes under **More tools** (⋯), always last.

1. Open **More tools** > **Customize quick bar**.
2. Drag a tool or a separator to where you want it. The bar follows at once.
3. **Reset to default** puts everything back.

A tool keeps its number key wherever you put it. The order is kept on this
device only.

## Good to know

- Keys work only when you're not typing in a text box.
- The number keys go with the tools, not with their places on the bar.
- Your bar's order doesn't follow you to another device or browser.
- **Other Traces** and **Your Pinterest Boards** have no key.
