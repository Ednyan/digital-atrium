# Templates

Ready-made sets of traces, kept in folders. Place one, then change it as you like:
the template stays as it was.

![The Templates sidebar: folders of templates, each with a small plan of itself, and Quick at the foot](../images/templates-panel.png)

## Place a template

Open **Templates** in the top bar.

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| It in a chosen place | Drag it onto the canvas |
| It in the middle of your view | Click it |

**Ctrl+Z** removes the whole template in one step.

A template with values or slots is placed as one [group](../arranging/groups-and-layers.md):
click it to move it whole, double-click a trace in it to change that trace.
The others are placed as loose traces.

## Fill a slot

A slot is a dashed box waiting for a picture. Drag a picture, a trace or a group
onto it **holding Ctrl** (⌘ on a Mac), and let go: it takes the slot's place and size.

| The slot says | What you put in it |
|---|---|
| **Fill** | Covers the slot; a picture is cropped to its shape |
| **Fit** | Shown whole, inside the slot |
| **Stretch** | Stretched to the slot's shape |

To take it out, drag it away **holding Ctrl**. Without Ctrl, it snaps back.

## Change a template's values

Some templates keep **values**: one setting shared by several traces, such as
the colour of every box in a mind map.

1. Click the template to select it.
2. At the top of the Customization panel, under **Values**, change a value.
3. Every trace linked to it follows, in one step of **Ctrl+Z**.

Changing a linked setting on one trace changes the value too, and the others
follow. In the **Colour palette**, typing a new code under a swatch recolours it.

### Rules

A value can **follow** another of its kind by a rule. It's then set by its rule,
and changes when the one it follows does:

| Rule | For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Lighter than** / **Darker than** | Colours | Tint 1: 30% lighter than Base |
| **Readable on** | Colours | Text colour: near-black or white, whichever reads on Box colour |
| **Multiple of** / **Added to** | Numbers | Heading size: Body size × 2.5 |
| **Capitals of** | Text | The small name in a reference sheet |
| **Opposite of** | On/off | Shown when the other is hidden |

Under a value, a rule shows as **↳ rule, value it follows, amount**. Change any of
them there; **×** stops following, and the value keeps what it holds. A setting set
by a rule is greyed in the panel: change the value it follows instead.

## Keep your favourites to hand

Point at a template and press its **pin**. It moves to **Quick** at the foot of the
sidebar, where you drag or click it the same way. You can pin up to 8. Press the
pin again to take it off.

Signed in, your Quick templates are kept with your account, the same on every
device. Signed out, they're kept on this device.

## What's in the Digital Atrium folder

| Folder | Template | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Boards | **Moodboard** | A frame that arranges what you put in it into columns |
| Boards | **Task board** | To do, Doing and Done, a card in each; a card moved between them falls into place |
| Boards | **Storyboard** | Six numbered shots, each a Fill slot with a caption |
| Boards | **Before and after** | Two Fit slots side by side |
| Boards | **Retrospective** | Went well, To improve and Actions, arranged like the task board |
| Boards | **Weekly planner** | Seven days that arrange what you put in them |
| Diagrams | **Mind map** | A main idea joined to four more. Values: box colour, text colour (readable on it), font |
| Diagrams | **Flowchart** | Four steps joined by arrows. The mind map's values |
| Diagrams | **SWOT** | Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats, each arranging what you put in it |
| Diagrams | **Timeline** | Five events joined by arrows, a date above each. The mind map's values |
| Studies | **Reference sheet** | A name, Front, Side and Back Fit slots, and notes |
| Studies | **Study grid** | Nine Fill slots |
| Studies | **Colour study** | A base colour with two tints and two shades made from it by rules |
| Studies | **Type specimen** | One typeface at three sizes, kept in proportion by rules |
| Pieces | **Note** | A box of text |
| Pieces | **Colour palette** | Five colours, each linked to its code |
| Pieces | **Photo card** | A card with a Fill slot and a caption that stays readable on the card's colour |
| Pieces | **Book or film** | A Fit slot for the cover, a title, a rating and notes |

Their words come in your language, and their colours follow the atrium's look.

## Good to know

- You can't make your own templates yet.
- A template dropped on an arranged frame or a slot joins it. Dropped on a plain
  frame, it sits on top; move it a little to put it in.
- You need permission to edit the atrium: **Templates** doesn't show in view-only
  atriums.
