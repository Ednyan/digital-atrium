# Your first atrium

An atrium is an endless canvas for your pictures, links and notes. Here are the
five steps from an empty one to a shared one.

To be shown around the app instead, press **Tutorial**.

## 1. Make one

1. On **Atriums**, press **Create New**.
2. Name it, and choose **Where it's kept**: **Online**, **In this browser**, or
   in your Google Drive. On desktop, it can also be kept on this computer.
3. Press **Create Atrium**.

Where it's kept decides how you can share it and how much it can hold. See
[Where atriums live](where-atriums-live.md).

## 2. Bring things in

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pictures or files | Drag them onto the canvas |
| What you copied | **Ctrl+V** |
| A video or web page | Press **7**, click, paste the link |
| A note | Press **2**, click, type |

More: [Drop and paste](../bringing-things-in/drop-and-paste.md), [The quick bar](../bringing-things-in/quick-bar.md).

## 3. Arrange

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| Move or resize | Drag the trace, or its handles |
| Several at once | **Shift**+click, or **Shift**+drag round them |
| A titled area that holds them | Select them, right-click > **Wrap in Frame** |
| Its look | Right-click > **Customize** |

More: [Selecting](../arranging/selecting.md), [Frames](../arranging/frames.md), [The Customization panel](../making-it-look-right/customization-panel.md).

## 4. Save

Press the save button under the atrium's name, or **Ctrl+S**. Changes wait for it, and **Don't Save**
takes them all back. See [Undo and saving](../arranging/undo-and-saving.md).

## 5. Share

**Menu** > **Share**. An online atrium shares by link; one in your Google Drive,
with the people you add. See [Sharing an atrium](../working-together/sharing.md).

## Good to know

- Moving around: drag empty canvas to move the view, and use the wheel to zoom. **Shift+1** shows everything. See [Moving around](../moving-around/moving-around.md).
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes, even after saving.
- A browser atrium lives in this browser only: export it to keep a copy.
