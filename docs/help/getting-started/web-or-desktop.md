# Web or desktop

The same Atrium runs on the web, at digitalatrium.org, and as a desktop app for
Windows, macOS and Linux.

| | On the web | Desktop app |
|---|---|---|
| **To start** | Nothing to install | Download and install it. See [Installing the desktop app](install-desktop.md) |
| **An account** | Optional | Not needed |
| **Your atriums live** | In this browser, online, or in your Google Drive | In a folder on your computer, the vault |
| **Working with others** | Yes: online and Drive atriums, live | Signed in: the atriums shared with you |
| **Room** | Depends where each atrium lives | Your disk |
| **Without internet** | No | Yes, for the vault's atriums |
| **Phones and tablets** | Yes | No |
| **Interface sounds** | Off at first | On at first |

## Only in the desktop app

- [Overlay mode](../keeping-and-moving/overlay-mode.md): an atrium floating over your other windows.
- [Live files](../bringing-things-in/live-files.md): spreadsheets and decks that follow their original file.
- **Store Embeds Locally** and **Restore From Vault**. See [The desktop vault](../keeping-and-moving/desktop-vault.md).

## Move atriums between them

| From | To | How |
|---|---|---|
| The desktop app | Online, or your Google Drive | Its card's **⋯** > **Put online…** |
| Online or Drive | The desktop app | Its card's **⋯** > **Keep an offline copy** |
| Either | Either | Export it as an .atrium file, then import it on the other |

See [Moving atriums](../keeping-and-moving/moving-atriums.md), [Export](../keeping-and-moving/export.md) and [Import](../keeping-and-moving/import.md).

## Good to know

- One account works in both: sign in on the web, and the desktop app signs in through the site.
- A copy made with **Put online…** or **Keep an offline copy** is its own atrium from then on.
